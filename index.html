🗓️ A two-week plan

  • Days 1-3 — Reduction of Risk Potential. Quiz that filter, then eBook Ch 49 (IV Therapy) and Ch 53 (Airway Management), plus Ch 12 (Client Safety).
  • Days 4-5 — Pharmacological & Parenteral. Quiz that filter, then eBook Ch 46-48 and 51.
  • Days 6-7 — Basic Care & Comfort. Modules 6, 9, 13, 14 on the All Modules page.
  • Day 8 — Health Promotion. Growth and development, eBook Ch 18-25. Milestones are memorization; the slides help.
  • Days 9-11 — ❌ My Misses until the counter drops. Highest-yield thing on the site.
  • Days 12-13 — filter 🔴 Hard, then the graded course quizzes (📝 Course Quizzes).
  • Day 14 — cram sheet, roadmaps, sleep.
